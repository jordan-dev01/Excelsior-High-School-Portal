Our History

Excelsior High School was founded in 1931 in Kingston, Jamaica, by Mr. Aston E. Tomlinson. From its earliest days, the school has carried the motto "Age Animo — Do It With Thy Might" as a call to excellence, discipline, and service.

Over nine decades, Excelsior has grown from a small secondary institution into one of Jamaica's most respected high schools, consistently producing leaders in business, academia, sports, and public service.

Landmark Accomplishments

  • Multiple Jamaica Independent Schools Association (JISA) sports championships.
  • Consistent top-tier passes in CSEC and CAPE examinations.
  • National awardees in the Jamaica Cultural Development Commission (JCDC) arts competitions.
  • Alumni serving at the highest levels of government, medicine, and industry.
